No. 01 A reader for Hacker News iPhone & iPad

A better way to read Hacker News.

Native, fast, and intentionally unhurried. HackerWire is the HN client built for people who actually read the thread — not just the headline.

 Download on the App Store

The premise

Hacker News is one of the last places online where the comments are the point. HackerWire is a reader designed around that — threaded discussions that stay readable at depth, typography that doesn't fight you, and the deliberate absence of everything a news app usually piles on. No feeds to doom-scroll. No notifications begging for attention. Just stories and comments.

Six things it does, and does carefully.

§ 01 — 06
01

Native iOS, fast

Performance Written in Swift against UIKit. Stories open instantly, scrolling stays at 120 Hz on ProMotion, and there is no web view hiding in the basement. No splash ads, no interstitials, no "what's new" modal. The app opens to the top stories and nothing else.

02

Threaded comments, done right

Reading Depth lines you can actually follow. Collapse a branch with a single tap; long threads never trap you fifteen levels deep with no way out. Tap a reply arrow to jump straight back to the parent. The comments are the reason you are here, so they get the attention.

03

AI summaries, on demand only

Pro feature Tap once for a short summary of the article or the full discussion. Summaries are generated when you ask for them — never preemptively, never in a feed. Up to thirty per day. Cached locally so you don't pay twice to read the same thing.

04

Seventeen themes, including Pure Black for OLED

Appearance Warm paper, cool paper, solarized, sepia, newsprint, and a true-black mode that draws as little battery as the display allows. Pick a theme and the whole app respects it — chrome, comments, syntax highlighting in code blocks, every last divider.

05

Bookmarks & offline reading

Keep Save a story and its discussion for the flight, the subway, or the beach. Bookmarks sync to iCloud across your devices. Offline mode keeps the last week of saved articles available without a connection, typography and all.

06

The details, where they matter

Craft Swipe left to upvote, right to save. Pull-to-refresh with a subtle haptic. Long-press any link for a preview. Dynamic Type throughout. VoiceOver labels on every control. Keyboard shortcuts on iPad. The small things, done on purpose.
